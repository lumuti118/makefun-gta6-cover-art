Turn your own photos into a Vice City sunset poster — a multi-panel GTA-style cover with bold caption lettering, ready to post. Upload one photo or several, pick a layout, grade it sunset, neon, noir or palms, write your caption, and export a print-ready PNG. Everything runs in your browser and works offline; your photos are read locally and never uploaded anywhere.

## What it does

- **Any photo**: upload your own photos (or try the two bundled samples), fill several panels with one photo or spread several photos across them.
- **Crop and position**: drag a photo inside its panel to reposition it, scroll to zoom, or use the per-panel zoom slider and Centre button.
- **Panel layouts**: Hero + 2, Trio, Quad, and Full photo.
- **Caption**: main caption and sub-caption, size, four lettering colours, top or bottom placement — bold outlined poster lettering with a slight tilt.
- **Colour grades**: Sunset, Neon, Noir, and Palms (sunset grade with a drawn sun and palm silhouettes).
- **Fast export**: one click exports a PNG in post sizes 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350, or 1920 × 1080.

All artwork is drawn on a canvas with code — an original design with no Rockstar logos, artwork, characters, or fonts. Lettering uses bold system fonts, so the app needs no font files and makes zero network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open <http://localhost:8080> in a desktop browser.

No build step, no dependencies. Node 22 is enough.
